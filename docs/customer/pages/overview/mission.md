# Mission Control

## Purpose

Mission Control is the console's home page — a full-bleed fleet summary that answers "is
everything OK?" at a glance, with one-click paths into creating a machine or browsing your
existing VMs and hosts.

## When to use it

- As your landing page after logging in — check fleet health before diving into a specific
  resource.
- To quickly launch a new VM without navigating to the Virtual machines page first.
- To jump straight to Virtual machines or Hosts from a single click.

## How to get there

- Left rail: **Overview → Mission Control**
- This is a full-bleed page — the left rail and Inspector auto-collapse while you're on it. Click
  the panel-toggle icon (top-left, next to the Veyron wordmark) to bring the rail back if you need
  it without leaving the page.

## What you'll see

- A dark hero card with the Veyron wordmark, a one-word status line ("Operational." or an
  attention state), and a summary sentence — e.g. "2 of 2 machines running on 1 host. Nothing
  needs a look."
- A **Create a machine** pill button, plus **Virtual machines** and **Hosts** quick links, all in
  the hero.
- A stats strip below the hero: machine count, host count, free memory, and whether live
  migration is available on this cluster.
- A 5-dot colorway strip ("tone swatch") showing the accent colors used across the console's
  nav sections.

## What you can do

- Click **Create a machine** to open the new-VM form.
- Click **Virtual machines** or **Hosts** to jump to those pages.
